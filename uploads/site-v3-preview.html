About

Who you'd be hiring

I graduated from UC San Diego in 2026 with a degree in computer engineering. What I care about is the seam between hardware and software — where a datasheet claim meets a bench measurement and the two disagree.

Most of what I know came from getting things wrong in a way I could measure. I sent a PCB to fabrication with three defects in it and fixed all three by hand rather than lose a week of a ten-week schedule. I shipped a model that benchmarked at 89% and later proved it was really 78%. Those are the projects I'd rather talk about than the ones that went smoothly.

I'm looking for a firmware or embedded role on a team that treats security and careful work as defaults rather than as features.
